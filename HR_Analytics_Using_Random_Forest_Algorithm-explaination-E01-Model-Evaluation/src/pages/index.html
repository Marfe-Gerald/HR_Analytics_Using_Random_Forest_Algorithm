Model Performance

RMSE
14.6%
Our model has an RMSE of 0.146, which means it was wrong for only 51 of the 2,399 employees we tested. This is close to 0, so the model makes very few mistakes.
R² Score
84.6%
Our model has an R² score of 0.846, which is close to the perfect score of 1. This means it is much better than a simple guess at telling who will stay and who will leave.
